# 🚀 Angular Interview Questions

---

## 🔹 General

1. What is Angular and what are its main features?
2. What is Angular CLI and why is it important?
3. What is a module in Angular, and what is its role in an application?
4. How can components communicate with each other (e.g., @Input/@Output, services)?

---

## 🔹 Components

1. What are components in Angular and how are they structured?
2. How do you configure a component's selector, template, and style?
3. What are the main lifecycle hooks in Angular? (ngOnInit, ngOnChanges, ngOnDestroy)
4. What is two-way data binding in Angular?
5. What is the difference between ElementRef and Renderer2?
6. What is the difference between Default and OnPush change detection strategies?

---

## 🔹 Directives

1. What are directives in Angular and their types?
2. How do structural directives like *ngIf and *ngFor work?
3. What is the difference between \*ngIf and [hidden]?
4. How do you create a custom directive?

---

## 🔹 Pipes

1. What is a Pipe in Angular?
2. What is the difference between pure and impure pipes?
3. How do you create a custom pipe?

---

## 🔹 Routing

1. What is Routing in Angular and why is it used?
2. How do you pass parameters via routes and retrieve them?
3. What are Route Guards and how do you implement CanActivate?
4. How does lazy loading work in Angular?

---

## 🔹 RxJS

1. What is RxJS and how is it used in Angular?
2. What is the difference between Observable and Promise?
3. What are Subjects and BehaviorSubjects?
4. How do you unsubscribe from Observables properly?

---

## 🔹 Dependency Injection

1. What is Dependency Injection in Angular?
2. What is the difference between `providedIn: 'root'` and providers in NgModule?
3. What are useClass, useValue, and useFactory?

---

## 🔹 Forms

1. What is the difference between Template-driven Forms and Reactive Forms?
2. What are FormControl and FormGroup?
3. How do you implement custom validation in forms?
4. What is asynchronous validation?

---

## 🔹 HTTP

1. How do you make HTTP requests with HttpClient?
2. What is an HttpInterceptor and why is it used?
3. Why is the request cloned in an interceptor?

## 🔹 Unit Testing

1. What is TestBed and how is it used?
2. How do you test Angular components?
3. How do you mock services in Angular tests?

## 🔹 Signals (Angular 16+)

1. What are Signals in Angular?
2. How do Signals affect change detection?
